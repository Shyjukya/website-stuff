In this case I am willing to work out a payment plan with the commissioner. A down payment of at least £155 must still be made upfront to lock in a slot before I start working. This amount is non-refundable.All my prices listed are a base and may increase due to character and/or prop complexity.For PayPal payment I prefer friends \& family transfer, because it's safer for me as an artist. Other payment methods on PayPal are discussed individually.\## Cancellation \& RefundsIf the commissioner decides to cancel, I will issue a refund equivalent to the amount of work not done. If you need to cancel before sending information or payment, please let me know as soon as possible.I reserve the right to cancel the commission and issue a refund equivalent to the amount of work not done due to harassment, improper conduct, or poor communication.\## Work processI reserve the right to interpret your character in my own style. This means slightly tweaking colours and adjusting markings to better fit the shape language I use as necessary. In no case I will change things in such a way the character is unrecognizable.For fully rendered or full illustrations, I send over a sketch at no extra charge where you will be able to do one complete redo and a round of minor edits. Once it's coloured I'll take a round of small changes just to cover anything that I might have missed; markings for example. Any change beyond this phase so far is not available except for an additional charge.I accept changes based on things that I have forgotten or missed, such as markings, horns or accesories. This comes at no extra fee as long as the information was included in the original reference sheet or description in the commission form.\## TurnaroundDepending on my schedule, health, workload and the complexity of the commission, turnaround may range from a few weeks to even a month or two. If you need a commission done by a specific date, please specify that you need it done by a certain deadline. It is best to request a deadline at least two weeks in advance, and more time is always better, especially if the commission is large. I can give only a particular refund if this happens.\## Rights \& DistributionThe prices described here are for personal commissions and as such, I the artist, retain all copyright.
